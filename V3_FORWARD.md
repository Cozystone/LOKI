# Paired forward intervention after the main confirmation

The primary model and its 3501–3512 scores are frozen. Whole-trajectory MSE mixes heading, translation, recent appearance and room-mean effects. To isolate commanded translation, use untouched rooms 3901–3912, the same 12-room pretraining and 128-step lit adaptation, then four branches per room from identical visible prefixes. For each 20-step pair, choose 4, 8, 12 or 16 distinct ticks for forward in one arm and use in the other. The remaining randomized left/right/use commands are shared. Alternate treatment order. Keep the world, estimator and Inner starting states identical across the two arms and provide no sensory input during the forecast. Compare MSE of the **difference between the two final pictures** against zero, Inner and v2. Require at least 10% reduction and wins on 10/12 rooms against each. Include an oracle-free, action-independent zero-difference predictor and report the fraction of exactly identical true endpoint pairs.

For the 256-tick heat intervention, train an additional v3 on the same confirmation data with only its exponential modes active from the start. This diagnostic separates the effect of the extra oscillatory modes. It leaves the main acceptance tests unchanged.
