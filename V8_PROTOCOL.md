Conjunctive keys should separate opposite responses to the same action. Read the same recursive least-squares field and leverage score as v7. Train one twin on laptop, the other on glasses; alternate twin assignments. Independent camera pose/exposure/noise and sensor perturbations on queries. Both twins must be learned. Query each direction on destination and first memories again on return. Challenge with same-looking unseen devices with independent action-response law. Balance actions, test novel executions of familiar laws, also shuffle context among objects. Never compare historical exposure directly to different test conditions. Verify old v7 fields can cross upgrade and compare after additional learning. Identical observable histories admit no disambiguation.
