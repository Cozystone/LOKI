# v4 preregistration

Freeze before implementing or measuring v4. The hypothesis is that a matched-arm readout trained on the signed change of each retinal column can separate translation from the much larger appearance shared by both arms. Resolve command differences along the changing viewing direction, condition the response on local retinal context and a learned turning chart, enforce sign reversal when swapping arms. All retained coefficients, precision and motion state must fit in a single float32 matrix of at most 262144 bytes. Never supply true poses, depths, action semantics, room layouts or held-out branch outcomes to the predictor.

Use the v3 true-idle protocol, 20 blind steps, four pairs (4/8/12/16 substitutions) at lit prefix lengths 128/168/208/248. Alternate treatment order. Pre-select unseen room seeds 5101–5112. Room mean signed endpoint-difference MSE is the unit. Require at least 10% lower mean MSE and strictly lower error in at least 10 of 12 rooms against each of zero, Inner and frozen v3 (6d689b5). Also report whether wins strictly exceed 10. Never exclude identical endpoints. Report nonzero/identical pairs separately and actual counts.

Use only rooms 1–12 for learning. Training may include paired active interventions at independently drawn states in those rooms. Expose matched training sensory data to Inner and v3 controls, report both the historical and matched-training controls, and train an Inner with the paired endpoint loss. The held-out room receives only its factual lit trajectory. The judge may fork the simulator; the organ receives numeric commands and sensations. The deployed pair estimator is a dedicated read-only operation. Separate its effect from ordinary endpoint subtraction and report a matched-input ablation. Use earlier v3 rooms 4001–4012 for development only. Revisions after inspecting the new rooms require a new preregistration and independent seeds.

Re-run the v3 plain first-scene test (522 and 3701–3712) and heat interventions (3801–3808). Require first-scene MSE <=0.010 and <=1.10 times v3, thermal difference MSE <=1.10 times v3, >=90% correct signs and original >=50%/7-of-8 wins vs controls. These are separate wiring configurations. Report storage and learned output without a claim about hardware temperature or processing arbitrary histories losslessly.
